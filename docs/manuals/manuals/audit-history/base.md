# Audit History — User Manual (Base)

## 1. Purpose
Describe the purpose of the Audit History module and what business outcomes it supports.

## 2. Audience Applicability
This base manual applies to all audiences with access to Audit History. Role-specific variations are documented in audience-specific manuals.

## 3. Prerequisites
1. You have a valid account with access to Audit History.
2. Your tenant is active.
3. You have the required permissions for the tasks described below.

## 4. Navigation
1. Sign in to NucleiQ.
2. From the left sidebar, click Audit History.
3. Verify the Audit History landing page is visible.

**Screenshot placeholder:**
![Audit History landing page](../screenshots/audit-history/base/01-landing.png)

## 5. Core Tasks (Click-by-Click)

### 5.1 View Records
1. From the Audit History landing page, click **List** (or the default list view).
2. Use the search box to enter a keyword.
3. Click **Search**.
4. Use the filter panel to refine results.
5. Click a record to open its details.

**Screenshot placeholders:**
![List view](../screenshots/audit-history/base/02-list.png)
![Filter panel](../screenshots/audit-history/base/03-filters.png)
![Record detail](../screenshots/audit-history/base/04-detail.png)

### 5.2 Create a New Record
1. On the Audit History landing page, click **Add New**.
2. Complete all required fields (marked with *).
3. Upload any attachments, if applicable.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Confirm the success banner appears.

**Screenshot placeholders:**
![Create form](../screenshots/audit-history/base/05-create.png)
![Success banner](../screenshots/audit-history/base/06-success.png)

### 5.3 Edit an Existing Record
1. Open the record from the list view.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Update the required fields.
4. Click **Save**.
5. Confirm the updated values appear.

**Screenshot placeholders:**
![Edit form](../screenshots/audit-history/base/07-edit.png)

### 5.4 Delete or Archive a Record
1. Open the record from the list view.
2. Click **Delete** (or **Archive** if soft-delete is enabled).
3. Confirm the action in the dialog.
4. Verify the record no longer appears in active lists.

**Screenshot placeholders:**
![Delete confirmation](../screenshots/audit-history/base/08-delete.png)

### 5.5 Export Data (If Enabled)
1. Click **Export** in the toolbar.
2. Choose the format (CSV/XLS/PDF).
3. Click **Download**.

**Screenshot placeholders:**
![Export dialog](../screenshots/audit-history/base/09-export.png)

## 6. Validation and Error Handling
1. If a field is invalid, read the inline validation message.
2. Correct the value and click **Save** again.
3. If the issue persists, contact your administrator.

**Screenshot placeholder:**
![Validation error](../screenshots/audit-history/base/10-validation.png)

## 7. Audit and Activity Logs (If Enabled)
1. Open the record details.
2. Click **History** or **Audit Log**.
3. Review timestamps and user actions.

**Screenshot placeholder:**
![Audit log](../screenshots/audit-history/base/11-audit.png)

## 8. Troubleshooting
- If you cannot access Audit History, request access from your administrator.
- If the page is blank, refresh and re-login.
- If uploads fail, verify file type and size.

## 9. Support
Contact your administrator or the platform support team for assistance.
